Shenzhen — Lead story

THREE NUCLEAR POWERS, ONE ROOM

Trump, Xi and Putin hold the first-ever trilateral summit on the sidelines of the APEC meeting in Shenzhen. After 90 minutes the three leaders issue a four-point joint declaration — on UN reform, oil supply, state immunity and a pledge that “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.” No deal is reached on Ukraine.

Sources: The White House · AP · AFP

UN COURT: MYANMAR COMMITTED GENOCIDE

The International Court of Justice rules 9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya — the first such ruling by the world’s highest court.

Source: International Court of Justice · The Guardian
Washington — Congress

SENATE REJECTS IRAN WAR-POWERS LIMIT

The US Senate votes 51–48 against a measure to limit President Trump’s war powers in Iran, three Republicans backing it and one Democrat opposing.

Source: Associated Press

ANTHROPIC SOARS IN RECORD STOCK DEBUT
